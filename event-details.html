Tech Talks 2026

Date: 12 Nov 2026, 4:00 PM

Featured

Tech

About the event

Tech Talks 2026 is an evening of short talks from engineers and researchers on AI, chips and space tech. Each speaker has 15 minutes to share what they are working on, followed by a Q&A and a networking session with snacks.

Agenda

  • 4:00 PM: Doors open and welcome
  • 4:15 PM: Talk 1, AI in everyday apps
  • 4:45 PM: Talk 2, How modern chips are designed
  • 5:15 PM: Coffee break
  • 5:35 PM: Talk 3, Building small satellites
  • 6:05 PM: Open Q&A with all speakers
  • 6:30 PM: Networking and closing
